HOW TO PLAY

  1. Goal: keep Network Health above 0% until the timer ends. On level 4, beat the boss.
  2. Your phone runs an access point. Keep its load bar out of the red.
  3. Too busy? PUSH traffic to a neighbour or MOVE a big device away. BOOST = +50% for 5 s.
  4. FIX revives crashed APs and zaps bots. When the boss attacks, everyone taps FIX.
  5. No crashes = bigger streak = more points. Beat the high score!
